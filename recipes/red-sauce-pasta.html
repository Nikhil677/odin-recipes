Red Sauce Pasta Recipe

Home page

Description

I belong to a indian household. So I don't use cheese that is usually used. But I can guarantee you that it will sure taste good.

Now lets start with the ingredients for the indian style red sauce pasta.

Ingredients

  • Pasta(Elbow/Straight/Spring) - 1 bowl (boiled)
  • Pasta water (water which was used to boil pasta)
  • Cheese - 1 to 2 cubes (I use amul btw)
  • Tomatoes - 5 to 6 (chopped medium)
  • Onion - 1 (chopped thin)
  • Cooking oil - 3 tbsp
  • Red chilli flakes - 1 to 2 tbsp
  • Red chilli dried (not powder) - 2
  • Salt - as per taste
  • Coriander/Cilantro
  • Garlic - 4 to 5 cloves

Procedure

Paste preparation

  1. Add oil and let it heat.
  2. Add garlic cloves and dried red chilli and cook until slight golden brown cloves.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook for 5 to 10 minutes until they start to melt in high heat.
  4. Add salt and cook until tomatoes melt enough you can see the paste not completely but nearly half of the volume from initial state.
  5. Let it cool.
  6. Grind it in the mixture.

Paste is ready!

Final cooking

  1. Add oil or ghee and let it heat.
  2. Add rai and jeera(optional)
  3. Add Chopped onions and cook little add salt and cook until transparent or golden brown
  4. Add the tomato paste and let it heat.
  5. Add grated cheese and pasta water and cook until it starts to boil and thickens
  6. Add the pasta and mix and cook for few minutes until you feel the pasta has absorbed the paste a little

Voila! Your Red Sauce Pasta is ready to be served!!!
