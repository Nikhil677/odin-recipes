Dry Fruit Ladoo Recipe

Home page

Description

I love this laddoo. Its healthy and sweet. This laddoo satisfies the desire of eating something sweet without effecting our health. I love this laddoo.

Now lets start with the ingredients for this Healthy snack.

Ingredients

  • Cashew nuts - 1 bowl
  • Almonds - 1.5 bowl
  • Walnuts - 1/2 bowl
  • Lion Dates - 1 bowl
  • Pista - 1/2 bowl
  • Seeds(Chia, pumpkin, sunflower, etc) - optional
  • Makhana

Procedure

Dry fruit pre-preparation

  1. Add ghee 1 tbsp and let it heat in pan.
  2. Add all listed ingredients except dates in the pan.
  3. Cook it until slight golden brown.
  4. Spread it in a plate and let it cool.

Laddoo preparation

  1. In a Grinder mixer add all the ingredients except dates.
  2. Grind it until it is just half grinded. Off it.
  3. Add the dates and then grind again until the paste is sticky.
  4. Now take the grinded ingredients on a plate
  5. Start making laddoos with it

Tasty and healthy Laddoos are ready!!
